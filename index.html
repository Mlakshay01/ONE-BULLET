Real 2-player multiplayer — one person hosts, the other joins with a single 4-digit code (peer-to-peer, no account needed). Each player plays on their own device. Desktop: A / D move, Space jump, mouse aims & shoots. Phone: use the on-screen arrows and JUMP button to move, drag anywhere on the arena to aim, then lift your finger to fire. You carry one bullet — fire it and you're empty until you reload. Bullets ricochet once off a wall or the arena edge, then gravity always pulls them down to rest on the ground. Walk up to a spent bullet to reload, or if you're unarmed, fire near one to kick it away instead.
